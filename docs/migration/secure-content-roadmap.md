## Publication, rollback and maintenance checklist for future approval

- Assign content/rights reviewer and operator; require provenance/rightsRef, safe text review, attribution and expiry per item, maximum20/category. Protect publication changes; gateway identity cannot write.
- Review demand, pool age/novelty and aggregate alerts weekly during the first month, then monthly if stable. Recheck provider terms, quota/billing and access monthly and before any change.
- Keep edge/public budget exhaustion safe:429/503 and no public provider refill. Test counter failure/races, license loss, provider outage, cache purge and budget cutoff before release.
- Preserve mock provider and last approved safe artifact; rollback gateway version, disable ingestion and purge affected content. A new mobile build/release needs its own approval; no hidden remote feature-control service is planned.
- Never restore revoked credentials or withdrawn content during rollback. Already delivered public text is not remotely erasable; rights/retention must reflect that limitation.
- Preserve original native/backup and owner state. No regeneration, Pods/signing/identity/permission changes or destructive cleanup in these scopes unless separately proposed and approved.
